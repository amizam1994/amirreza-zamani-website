I’m Amirreza Zamani. I develop mathematical tools for privacy and fairness in data disclosure, learning, and communication.

Point-wise privacy leakage

Featured contribution

Point-wise f-divergence
privacy leakage

I introduced point-wise f-divergence privacy leakage measures to the privacy community through my work on strong χ² and ℓ₁ privacy. These measures impose a leakage bound on every possible release. Even a rare output must satisfy the privacy guarantee.

Strong χ² privacy · TIFS 2021Strong ℓ₁ privacy · TIFS 2022

Leakage for a single release

Df(PX|U=u ‖ PX)

Seeing u changes an observer’s belief about the private variable X. The divergence quantifies how much that belief changes for this particular outcome.

Featured contribution

Extended functional
representation lemmas

I introduced the Extended Functional Representation Lemma (EFRL) and Extended Strong Functional Representation Lemma (ESFRL), extending the FRL and SFRL to allow bounded leakage. I have used these tools in privacy, fairness, compression, semantic communication, and learning.

Research contribution

Perfectly private
lossless compression

I study variable-length coding when the useful data must be recovered without disclosing a correlated private attribute. My work develops bounds and constructive schemes for the expected code length. The Entropy paper treats zero and nonzero privacy leakage; the JSAIT paper studies lossless coding with a sequential encoder under perfect privacy.

Research contribution

Private semantic
communication

I design messages that preserve information needed for a task while limiting what they reveal about private data. My multi-task work studies how to serve several tasks under a bounded leakage constraint. Related conference papers develop private semantic schemes for multiple tasks and for separate blind encoders.

Research contribution

Private and fair
learning algorithms

With Abolfazl Changizi, I study fair representations that retain task information under demographic parity and rate constraints. My learning work also develops generalization bounds for adaptive learners under privacy and fairness constraints. With Raghav Bongole, I study risk bounds for interactive decisions, including minimax quantiles and conditional value-at-risk under local differential privacy.

Academic background

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Information Science and Engineering division at KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm. My research uses information theory to study privacy, fairness, semantic communication, and learning. I work on both fundamental limits and constructive mechanisms.

I completed my PhD in Electrical Engineering at KTH in 2023. My thesis, Privacy Mechanism Design Through the Lens of Information Theory, brought together problems in compression, caching, and semantic communication. Earlier, I studied electrical engineering at Sharif University of Technology and the University of Tehran.
